# Five lines. Two phones camp on the same busy line, then a third
# starts 01 and the call ends before the 1.
# A tap on the phone body hooks an idle, ringing, or recall phone off,
# and hooks any other phone on.
tap lines-5
tap open

# Phone 1 calls phone 2, and phone 2 answers.
tap phone-1
tap digit-1-2
see Calling 2
tap phone-2
see 1 and 2
see In call with 2

# Phones 3 and 4 both camp on phone 1.
tap phone-3
tap digit-3-1
see Busy signal
tap digit-3-0
tap digit-3-1
see Waiting for 1
see 3 is waiting for 1
tap phone-4
tap digit-4-1
see Busy signal
tap digit-4-0
tap digit-4-1
see 4 is waiting for 1

# Phone 1 hangs up. Phone 3 rings first. Phone 4 stays on the list.
tap phone-1
see 3 is ringing
see 4 is waiting for 1

# Phone 3 finishes that recall, then the call ends and phone 4 rings.
tap phone-3
tap phone-3
see 1 is ringing
tap phone-1
see 1 and 3
tap phone-3
see 4 is ringing
tap phone-4
tap phone-4
see 1 is ringing
tap phone-1
see 1 and 4

# Phone 5 hears busy, presses 0, and the call ends before the 1.
# The 1 still camps on, and phone 5 rings at once because line 1 is idle.
tap phone-5
tap digit-5-1
see Busy signal
tap digit-5-0
tap phone-1
tap digit-5-1
see 5 is ringing
see Calling 1
